About the project

Infinite Random Obby combines obby progression with a growing collection of themed environments. The archive contains Halloween, ice, sakura, swamp and market artwork, alongside checkpoint graphics, double-jump boosts and Coinfinite assets. Versioned Roblox backups and development notes document speedrun leaderboards, badges and a music system. The artwork below includes actual gameplay screenshots.
